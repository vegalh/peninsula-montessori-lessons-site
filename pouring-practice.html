Pouring Practice

2–3 years · Practical Life · 8 minutes

Children build concentration and fine motor control by pouring water carefully between a pitcher and cups.

✋ Fine Motor
🎯 Concentration
⚙ Independence
💧 Care of Self

Materials

How to Present

  1. Fill the pitcher partway with water and place it on the tray next to an empty cup.
  2. Show the child how to hold the pitcher with both hands and pour slowly.
  3. Invite the child to pour water into the cup, then pour it back into the pitcher.
  4. Encourage the child to wipe up any spills with the sponge.

Notes

Builds concentration and fine motor control. Start with a small amount of water to reduce spills.
