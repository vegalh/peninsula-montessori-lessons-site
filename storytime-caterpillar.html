Storytime: The Very Hungry Caterpillar

2–3 years · Language · 15 minutes

Children build early literacy and vocabulary by following along with the story and matching felt fruit props to each page.

💬 Vocabulary
🎯 Concentration
👥 Group Time
🐛 Sequencing

Materials

How to Present

  1. Gather children in a circle and introduce the caterpillar puppet.
  2. Read the story slowly, holding up each fruit prop as it's mentioned.
  3. Invite children to name each fruit before you turn the page.
  4. After the story, retell the sequence of fruits together as a group.

Notes

Great for building sequencing skills and group listening. Works well as a circle-time opener.
