Leaf Transfer

2–3 years Practical Life Fall

◙ 10 min □ 3 materials

Materials Required

  • Small tongs or tweezers
  • Assorted dried leaves (various sizes)
  • Two small bowls

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Set up a tray with one bowl of leaves on the left and an empty bowl on the right.
  2. Show the child how to grip the tongs and pick up a single leaf.
  3. Transfer the leaf to the empty bowl, one at a time, until all leaves are moved.
  4. Invite the child to reverse the transfer back to the original bowl.

Notes

Great for fine motor development and hand-eye coordination. Supervise closely while tongs are in use.
