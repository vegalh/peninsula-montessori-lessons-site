Color Mixing

3–4 years Science Colors

◙ 15 min □ 5 materials

Materials Required

  • Clear cups (6)
  • Red, blue, and yellow liquid watercolor
  • Pipettes or droppers
  • Water
  • Tray to contain spills

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Fill three cups with water and add one primary color to each.
  2. Set out three empty cups for mixing.
  3. Show the child how to use a pipette to draw up color and drop it into an empty cup.
  4. Invite the child to combine two primary colors and observe the new color that forms.

Notes

Wear smocks or work near a sink. Great for introducing basic color theory vocabulary (primary, secondary).
