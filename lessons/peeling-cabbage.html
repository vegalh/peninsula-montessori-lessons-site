Peeling Cabbage

2–3 years · Practical Life · 10 minutes

Children build fine motor control and sensory awareness by peeling leaves off a whole cabbage one at a time.

✋ Fine Motor
🎯 Concentration
⚙ Independence
🌿 Nature

Materials

How to Present

  1. Invite the child to the tray and name the cabbage.
  2. Demonstrate gently peeling one leaf at a time and placing it in the bowl.
  3. Invite the child to continue peeling leaves at their own pace.
  4. Talk about the colors and textures of the leaves as you go.

Notes

A simple, repeatable sensory activity that builds fine motor control and focus.
