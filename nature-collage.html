Nature Collage

3–5 years Art Fall

◙ 20 min □ 6 materials

Materials Required

  • Construction paper
  • Non-toxic glue sticks
  • Assorted leaves, twigs, and flower petals
  • Small basket for collected materials
  • Scissors
  • Tray to contain mess

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Take a short walk to collect leaves, twigs, and petals together.
  2. Lay out the collected materials on a tray and let the child arrange them on paper.
  3. Show the child how to apply a small amount of glue and press each piece down.
  4. Let the collage dry flat before moving it.

Notes

A calm, open-ended art activity. Great for fine motor skills and appreciation of natural materials.
